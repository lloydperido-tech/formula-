CvSU Document Request Fees

Below is the complete fee schedule for various academic documents. Additional copies may incur separate charges. Payment is required at the CvSU Cashier Office.

Document Type First Copy Additional Copies Processing Time
Transcript of Records ₱50.00 ₱25.00 each 3-5 days
Certificate of Enrollment ₱50.00 ₱25.00 each 2-3 days
Certificate of Registration ₱50.00 ₱25.00 each 2-3 days
Diploma/Degree Certificate ₱100.00 ₱50.00 each 5-7 days
Good Moral Certificate ₱25.00 ₱15.00 each 2-3 days
Honorable Dismissal ₱50.00 ₱25.00 each 3-5 days
Course Description ₱50.00 ₱25.00 each 3-5 days
Certification of Units Earned ₱50.00 ₱25.00 each 3-5 days
Request Documents Now

Important Notes

  • All prices are in Philippine Pesos (₱)
  • Payment must be made at the CvSU Cashier Office
  • Prices are subject to change without notice
  • Rush requests may have additional charges
  • Bring your payment receipt when claiming documents

Payment Methods

Currently, payments are accepted at the CvSU Cashier Office during office hours:

  • Cash: Direct payment at the cashier
  • Bank Transfer: Via CvSU Official Account
